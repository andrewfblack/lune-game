How to Play

  • 🗣️ Talk to your friends — tap the glowing ▼ or press Space / Enter.
  • 🗺️ Explore the pillow fort. Your choices shape Lune's sleepover moments.
  • 📒 Add a sticker to Lune’s Memory Book for each completed episode. Use its chapter tabs to revisit your memories. Finishing a chapter’s episodes unlocks the next chapter.
  • 🎁 Find keepsakes, Bloopers and Settings in the hub’s Menu. Open Friends of the Fort for a portrait grid, then tap a friend for their character card. There are no battles or party selections.
  • 🎬 Collect bloopers for the Flop-O-Matic 3000. Choose which clips to save.
  • 🌟 Start with The First Ah in Chapter 1. The five-week sleepover follows in Chapter 2; Slime & Secrets is Chapter 3.
  • 💾 Your game autosaves during conversations. There is nothing extra to press.

Comfort Settings

Changes apply straight away and stay in this browser. Your story progress is unchanged.
